Welcome (or welcome back) to Nate's place. It is a new season in our lives here at The Ranch, an undisclosed place in the PNW. It is also a new season for this newly-renewed website:  Nate's Place.

Outside it is a new season, too. It seems very much to be fall, at least according to the sugar maple tree outside our house, but some plants -- at least one plant -- may be a little confused.

![A Confused Crocus](images/crazy_crocus.jpg "A Confused Crocus")

As I stepped out the door the other day (to get some firewood, I believe) I saw a flower growing up through the soil between the stepping stones. Though it was already the beginning of October, there was a spring flower growing and blooming there, a crocus!

I have not researched as to the cause of this unusual event, but it may just be that the little plant got confused by the beautiful, warm weather that we had been having. Or perhaps it was the Lord reminding us of how beautiful His creation is.

Whatever the cause, I love to see the beautiful, tender little crocus flowers in March or April around here; and I am extra pleased to see such a beauty out of season.

Until next time.
